How to play

Every letter in the quote has been swapped for another, and the swap is the same all the way through. If K stands for T once, it stands for T everywhere. No letter stands for itself. Tap a cell to pick its cipher letter, then tap the letter you think it really is. One-letter words and double letters are good places to start.

A hint locks in one letter. A check marks your wrong guesses in red. Both count against your score when you share it.
